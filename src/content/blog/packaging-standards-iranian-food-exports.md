Packaging is the export discipline that determines whether a food product arrives at destination as the product the buyer ordered, or as a degraded version of it. Iranian food exports — saffron, pistachios, dates, dried fruit, almonds — each have a packaging spec that protects the product's specific failure mode: saffron loses aroma to oxygen and UV; pistachios are at aflatoxin risk from moisture; Mazafati dates degrade at ambient temperature; dried fruit is hygroscopic and absorbs humidity. This guide is the packaging framework we apply to every Iranian food export, and it is the framework we recommend buyers require from any Iranian food supplier they evaluate.

## Saffron packaging: argon-flushed, aluminium-lined, light-blocked

Saffron's three commercially relevant compounds — crocin (colour), picrocrocin (flavour), safranal (aroma) — are all degraded by oxygen, moisture and UV light. The export-grade packaging standard we apply for [bulk Super Negin saffron for European importers](/export/iranian-saffron-super-negin/):

1. **Primary pack**: food-grade aluminium-lined triple-laminated pouch. The aluminium layer is the oxygen and UV barrier; the inner food-grade layer is the food-contact surface; the outer layer is the print and mechanical layer.
2. **Gas flush**: nitrogen or argon flush to displace oxygen in the headspace. Argon is denser than nitrogen and is our default for high-value Super Negin because it provides a marginally better oxygen displacement; nitrogen is the cost-effective default for Sargol and Negin volumes.
3. **Heat seal**: a full-width heat seal, not a clip or a zip closure, because the seal integrity is what maintains the gas flush.
4. **Secondary pack**: labelled carton with batch number, harvest year, ISO 3632 values, and net weight, matching the commercial invoice line.
5. **Tertiary pack**: corrugated export carton, 1-5 kg per carton, palletised for full-container orders.

Under this packaging, saffron holds its ISO 3632 values for 24-30 months at ambient warehouse temperature below 25°C, away from direct sunlight. The shelf life is the packaging spec, not a number printed on a label — a buyer who accepts vacuum packaging without the aluminium lining is accepting a shorter shelf life and a higher risk of safranal loss in transit.

Vacuum packaging is available on request but is not our default. Over-vacuum can fracture the saffron threads and reduce the visual grade — a real concern for Super Negin and Negin, where the thread length and intactness are the visual grade. For Sargol and industrial-grade saffron, vacuum is acceptable because the visual grade is not the buyer's concern.

## Pistachio packaging: the moisture-management decision

Pistachio packaging is a moisture-management decision. The product ships at 6-7% moisture — the threshold below which Aspergillus mould cannot grow — and the packaging must maintain that moisture through the transit and destination-storage environment. The options, as covered in the [Iranian pistachio quality standards](/blog/iranian-pistachio-quality-standards-aflatoxin/) guide:

- **50 kg PP woven bags (in-shell)**: the default for industrial and [Iranian pistachio supplier for Russia](/export/bulk-fandoghi-pistachios-wholesale/) export. Breathable, low packaging cost, suitable for dry-container shipping. The bag breathes; the nuts' 6-7% moisture is maintained if the container stays dry. The right choice for dry destination ports (Moscow overland, Riyadh) and for buyers who will process the nuts within weeks of arrival.
- **25 kg vacuum-sealed cartons (in-shell or kernel)**: the format for higher-value lots and kernel exports. Vacuum protects the kernel from oxidation; the carton protects the vacuum. The right choice for humid destination ports (Mumbai, Jakarta, Lagos) where condensation is a risk, and for kernel exports where the cut surface of the kernel is oxidation-sensitive.
- **12.5 kg foil pouches (kernel)**: nitrogen-flushed, the format for the [pistachio butter bulk supply wholesale](/export/bulk-fandoghi-pistachios-wholesale/) market and the slicing market. The kernel arrives in condition for direct processing.

The packaging choice is a destination-climate decision and a buyer-handling-capability decision. A buyer shipping to a humid destination in PP bags is a buyer whose nuts may absorb humidity in transit and breach the 7% moisture threshold; a buyer with vacuum cartons is protected. The packaging spec belongs on the proforma, not in a post-shipment email.

## Mazafati date packaging: selective layering and reefer-compatible cartons

Mazafati date packaging is a cold-chain-compatible and presentation decision, as covered in the [shipping dates from Iran cold-chain](/blog/shipping-dates-from-iran-cold-chain/) guide:

- **Selective-layered 10 kg cartons**: the premium export format. Dates are hand-layered flat with separator sheets; the carton is vented to allow cold air circulation through the reefer. 700-1,000 cartons per 20ft reefer. The format for [Mazafati dates Bam vs Jiroft wholesale](/export/mazafati-dates-bulk-import/) buyers whose customer opens the carton and finds a visually uniform, retail-ready product.
- **Bulk loose 10-12 kg cartons**: dates unsorted, tipped into the carton. Lower per-kg cost; the format for date paste, syrup and industrial buyers. Same venting, same cartons-per-container.
- **Gift trays and retail clamshells**: 1-5 kg formats for buyers who sell the dates in the packaging they ship in. Higher packaging cost; requires careful stacking to avoid crushing in the reefer.

The venting is the reefer-compatibility detail. A non-vented carton in a reefer container blocks cold air circulation, and the dates inside the carton do not cool to the reefer set-point — the reefer reads the set-point, the dates inside the carton read ambient, and the cargo degrades despite the reefer running. The venting is a packaging spec, not a carton-furniture detail.

## Dried fruit packaging: the hygroscopic question

Iranian dried fruit — figs, apricots, raisins, barberries, sour cherries — is hygroscopic: it absorbs humidity from the air. At low moisture (under 15%), dried fruit is shelf-stable at ambient temperature; at higher humidity it absorbs moisture, softens, and becomes mould-susceptible. The packaging standard:

- **10-12.5 kg food-grade cartons with inner liner**: the inner liner (a food-grade plastic liner or a vacuum pouch) is the moisture barrier. The carton is the mechanical protection.
- **Vac pouches for premium dried fruit**: vacuum-sealed foil pouches for higher-value dried fruit (premium figs, barberries for the European market) where the oxidation and moisture protection justifies the cost.
- **50 kg bags for industrial dried fruit**: for date paste, raisin paste, and industrial buyers, where the buyer processes the fruit on arrival and the packaging is a transport container, not a shelf-life barrier.

## Almond and gift-box packaging

Iranian almond packaging follows the same moisture and oxidation logic as pistachios, with the additional consideration that processed formats (blanched, sliced, slivered) have cut surfaces that are oxidation-sensitive:

- **Natural kernels**: 12.5 kg or 25 kg vacuum-sealed foil pouches in cartons; or 25 kg nitrogen-flushed food-grade bags.
- **Blanched / sliced / slivered**: 12.5 kg vacuum cartons — the vacuum is essential for the cut surfaces.
- **Gift boxes**: retailer-specified carton counts (6, 12, or 24 boxes per shipping carton) for the [saffron nuts gift box wholesale](/export/golden-almonds-iran-sourcing/) programme; palletised for full-container orders.

## Food-grade and certification compliance

All primary packaging materials we use are food-grade and compliant with the destination market's food-contact regulations: EU 10/2011 for European buyers, FDA 21 CFR for US-destined packaging (where applicable), and the relevant GCC and Asian food-contact standards. The compliance is documented with a food-contact declaration from the packaging supplier, and the declaration is available on request.

For organic-labelled products, the packaging chain is part of the organic certification — the packaging must not introduce non-organic contaminants — and the organic certification body audits the packaging workflow as part of the annual certification audit.

## Labelling and the spec-sheet-on-the-carton principle

The export carton label is the on-carton spec sheet. Our standard label carries: product name, grade, variety (where applicable), count or size, net weight, gross weight, batch number, harvest year, country of origin, and the supplier (Palude) identifier. The label is the document that allows the buyer's warehouse to receive the carton against the packing list without opening it, and it is the document that supports the lot traceability in the event of a recall or a quality dispute.

A buyer who receives cartons with no label, or with a label that does not match the packing list, is a buyer whose documentation chain has broken at the packaging step — and the break should be resolved with the supplier before the container is unloaded, not after.
